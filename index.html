TPlus eCampus

A timetable in the student's or lecturer's pocket. It pulls each person's own classes and exams from their institution's E-Campus system, shows the week and the master timetable, reminds them before a session, and stays readable offline and in dark mode. What makes it unusual is restraint: it is read-only by design, so a timetable can never be corrupted from a phone, and one app serves several institutions with each one's own login.

Every API endpoint answers in a median of under 35 ms, load testing finished with 0% failed requests, and the rate limit held to its budget exactly under a burst. The API contract passes the OWASP API Security Top 10 checks with zero findings. Testing on a real Android phone caught three reminder and widget defects the emulator had missed; all three were fixed.

UNIMAS Shuttle Bus Service Mobile Application

A campus shuttle app for students, drivers and administrators. Students see where every bus is on the map right now, check routes and schedules, reserve a seat and board with a QR code. Drivers share their live position. Administrators manage buses, destinations and timetables and can see how the service is used. Built alone as my final year project and tested with real students, it met its targets and was commended at the faculty symposium for the interface, the logic and the idea.

The requirements survey found 75% of students had missed a bus because they could not tell when it would arrive, and 100% wanted the schedule and seat availability on their phone. In acceptance testing after a live demonstration, 100% rated the tracking, QR boarding and travel planning Good or Very Good, and every functional test case passed.

Nasyrah Website and Poster Generator

Rumah Kebajikan Nasyrah is a welfare home and Quran education centre in Pekan Nanas, Johor, that shelters asnaf boys aged seven to fourteen and opens Quran classes to the community. I built its website in Malay: who they are, the home and its facilities, the Islamic studies programme, activities, how to join, how to donate and how to reach them. It is a plain static site that loads fast on any phone and still shows the contact details when JavaScript is off.

For its social media I built a poster generator. Staff paste an Arabic quote, its Malay translation and the source, and get back a finished poster on Nasyrah's template, with one click to copy it straight into WhatsApp; a batch mode renders a whole list of quotes at once.

Photos are 72% lighter than the originals, with location data stripped from every image of the home. Every page passes HTML validation with zero console errors, and the menu and photo viewer work fully from the keyboard. The poster layout lands within 0.5% of the hand-made originals, and the tool refuses a quote it cannot trim cleanly rather than guess where to cut.
